Our Story

A Coffee House Since the Age of Empire

Tucked beside the University of Oxford on High Street, Queen's Lane has been pouring coffee through more than three centuries of history — long before espresso machines, long before Wi-Fi, back when a cup of coffee was itself a strange and exciting new idea.

The cafe still keeps its original warren of cosy, low-ceilinged rooms, tucked in for scholars, travellers, and regulars alike. It's a place built for lingering — order slowly, stay a while, and let the centuries do the talking.
